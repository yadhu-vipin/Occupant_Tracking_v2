Building-Scoped Hierarchical RBAC

A > B > C (Dean > Teacher > Student)
A
Role A: Dean / Administrator Scoped to home building Br. Can inspect local occupants (L4 investigation, L2 routine) and visitors currently in Br (L2). Peer Deans at coarse status (L2). Cannot track B/C occupants in other buildings (L0).
B
Role B: Teacher / Faculty Scoped to home building Br. Can inspect assigned students in course roster during campus hours (L2). Cannot track occupants in other buildings (L0).
C
Role C: Student Self-query exemption (L4 full track). Staff cabin presence during office hours (L1 Boolean). Peer student queries denied (L0 anti-stalking isolation).
Visitor Predicate:
Visitor(p, b, t) ⇔ current_building(p,t) == b ∧ registered_building(p) ≠ b

Underlying database schemas are 100% unmodified; RBAC acts as disclosure-filtering middleware.
